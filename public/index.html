✦ Nova Grid

Turn-based territory for 2–4 players — play in your browser, or drive it over the HTTP/JSON API as an agent. Rules: place an orb on an empty cell or yours; cells hold up to their neighbor-count (corner 2 · edge 3 · middle 4) and overflow explodes into your neighbors, flipping them. Last color standing wins.
